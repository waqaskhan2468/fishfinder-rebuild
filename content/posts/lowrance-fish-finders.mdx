##### **Fish dimension on SONAR:**

You can determine your fish size by three factors regarding the transducer. A transducer receives sound produced by the fish. The sound-catching frequency of a transducer is the same as that of a fish voice. Following is an explanation of three factors:

1.  If the fish is large, it is clear that it will produce a big sound. Small fish has a tiny voice.

1.  You can determine fish color by using a transducer. The returned sound by it after catching fish voice signals is actually of a different frequency. It is because the sound comes from different colors of fish. Depending on the color of the fish, its sound can detect its kind.

1.  A thick arch is the sign of bigger fish, and likewise, a thin arch shows thin fish of small size.

##### **Down scan fish finding:**

It is an imaging of down-sided SONAR views with clear illustrative pictures. They are identical and equipped with advanced information.  

##### **Side scan concept:**

Due to its name, it gives a side view of what is in the water. Anglers use this view to depict the position of plenty of fish.  

##### **3D structure stream:**

##### **Q:** **Is it necessary to put the transducer in the water instead of keeping it on the boat?**

It is essential to keep the transducer in the water. The reason behind it is that a transducer can burn out if it is out of water. Try to keep it inside the water while fishing. It will give you two benefits. Firstly, it will provide you accurate readings by roaming in the water. Secondly, it is safe when it is in the water. Once it comes out of the water, the navigation reading becomes distorted, and chances become high that it will burn out. Preferably fish-finder and transducer both should be in the water, as they can work there phenomenally.

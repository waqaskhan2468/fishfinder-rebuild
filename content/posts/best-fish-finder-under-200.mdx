## What $200 actually buys you

At this price you're choosing between two different categories, not just different brands:

- **Mounted CHIRP units** (Garmin STRIKER, Humminbird PIRANHAMAX) — a fixed display plus a transom or trolling-motor-mounted transducer. Best sonar clarity for the money, but they need a boat or kayak with somewhere to mount them.
- **Castable/portable units** (Deeper START, STRIKER Cast, ReelSonar iBobber) — a wireless puck you cast out, paired to your phone. No mounting needed, so they work from shore, a dock, or any kayak — but the screen is your phone, and range/battery life are real limits.

What you give up compared to a $500+ unit: side imaging, larger screens, and — critically — none of these support live/forward-facing sonar (LiveScope, ActiveTarget, MEGA Live all start around $1,300 and need a compatible display). If that's what you're actually after, our [compatibility checker](/fish-finder-compatibility-checker/) will save you from an expensive mismatch.

The Striker Plus 4 is the STRIKER 4 with one meaningful addition: Quickdraw Contours, Garmin's built-in mapping that draws your own depth contour lines as you fish, in real time, on water with no preloaded chart. **Best for:** anyone with a boat or kayak who wants the most capability per dollar, full stop.

## 2. Garmin STRIKER 4 — Best for Beginners

If mapping isn't a priority — you fish the same familiar spots, or you just want sonar and depth — this is $20-30 cheaper for a very similar on-water experience.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is a fish finder under $200 actually good enough to find fish?**
Yes, for the core job — showing depth, bottom structure and fish arches — a $150-200 CHIRP unit performs well. What you lose at this price is side imaging, larger high-resolution screens, and any form of live/forward-facing sonar, not basic fish-finding accuracy.

**What's the difference between a mounted and a castable fish finder under $200?**
Mounted units (STRIKER 4, STRIKER Plus 4, PIRANHAMAX 4) need a transducer fixed to a boat or kayak and give the clearest, most stable sonar picture. Castable units (Deeper START, STRIKER Cast, iBobber) are wireless and cast out on your line, so they work from shore or a dock with no mounting at all, at the cost of using your phone as the screen.

**Do I need GPS on a budget fish finder?**
Only if you want to mark waypoints or build contour maps. The base STRIKER 4 and PIRANHAMAX 4 skip GPS to hit a lower price; if returning to the exact same spot matters to you, step up to the STRIKER Plus 4.

**Can I use any of these on a kayak?**
Yes — the mounted units need a way to fix the transducer (a suction mount or a drilled-in mount), while the castable units need nothing at all, which is why they're popular with kayak anglers who don't want to modify their boat.

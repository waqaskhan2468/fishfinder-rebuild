## **A Buying Guide to the Best Portable Fish Finder:**

#### **Working Mechanism of Fish Finder:**

A **portable fish finder** is a gadget/instrument that is utilized for the purpose of detecting the presence of fish and catching it instantly. It works on the devised principle of SONAR that is abbreviated as Sound Navigation Ranging. It observes the sound waves keenly. When the pulse of a sound wave is hit by any object in the sea, it gives a signal that is quickly received by fish finding instrument. After catching the object’s signal/message via sending pulse back, it tends to notice the nature of this object. Pulses are sent in and out of the deep water until the fish are detected. It is the same criteria used by dolphins to produce an echo for detecting danger or food around.

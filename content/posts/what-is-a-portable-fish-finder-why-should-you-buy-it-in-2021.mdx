The portable fish finder is a device which is using “Sonar Technology” to find the fish under the water. Sonar technology is used to sense the objects in the ocean under deep water. So, the portable fish finders uses this technology and detect the fishes under water in ocean and in deep water as well as shallow water.

![Portable Fish Finders](/images/2021/08/Portable-Fish-Finders-908x1024.jpg)

The old traditional fish finders needs to be wired on the boat but these new portable fish finders are very light weight. By using these gadgets, you don’t need to worry about the logistics and you can fish from the shore.

One most important and interesting thing about these portable fish finders is, you can keep them in your pocket like a mobile phone and even the large fish finders can fit in your small backpacks.

## How does a portable fish finder work?

As mentioned at the start of this article, fish finder devices use the sonar technology. The transducer keeps searching for any objects under the water. When the waves hit something, you will get a message with details on the screen of fish finder. If you are getting lot of hits on your portable fish finder’s screen, it means this is the best place for fishing.

![Sonar technology for portable fish finders](/images/2021/08/Sonar-technology-for-portable-fish-finders-1024x576.jpg)

Also, some of the best portable fish finders can help you find the exact information like how deep the fishes are and how many fished you can find from this spot.

## How to use a portable fish finder?

In general, every fish finder devices works in 3 steps and you can use it easily without any confusion.

-   Turn on the fish finder and adjust settings according to your requirements
-   Place the fishing line with attached transducer in the water
-   Keep your eyes on screen of fish finder to see fish detection on the screen

### **Technology**

Last but not least, you need to check out the technology. In the fish finders, you will get two technologies in general. Transducers and Sonar. Most Garmin gadgets have transducer technology. 

Let’s find out about these technologies in a bit more detail. 

#### **Transducer Technology** 

The technology works for the detection of fishes under the water surface. It sends frequencies in the 50-800 kHz range that helps in detection. Lower frequencies coming from the sounders penetrate well in the bottom and higher frequency gets details from the depth. 

#### **Sonar technology** 

Sonar technology works on the imaging of the details that comes up with the help of transducer technology. It works three ways: CHIRP sonar, SideVu, and Clear Vu. In most Garmin fish finders, you can find these three kinds of sonar options. 

These are the ultimate solutions to give you a clear and advanced image. High definition and clarity of details lets you get the best idea about what is going underwater. It helps in the determination of your targets and gets the most out of them.

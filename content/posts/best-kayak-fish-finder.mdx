#### **How Do Fish Finders Work?**

In simple words, a fish finder is a small electrical device that helps people locate fish underwater. Besides finding the fish underwater, it will help you indicate the depth of the water. Hence, you will find various pieces of information about the overall surroundings from this device.  
The **best kayak fish finder** has two main parts; a sonar transducer and a computer display. The sonar transducer does the real job of indicating the fish while you can see everything on the computer display. The process includes calculating time and the device completes with sound waves.

### **Sensitivity Unit – Gain – How to Set**

#### **How to change sensitivity to know you are seeing a fish in Garmin Fish Finder?**

The sensitivity unit in in portable Garmin fish finders is called “Gain”. Click on the gain and you will see the screen shown below in the image. Sensitivity on a Garmin sonar unit is extremely important when you are looking for an individual fish.

![How to use charter in garmin fish finders](/images/2021/09/Screenshot-212-1024x488.png)

Just remember that, the sensitivity unit is like a volume on the radio that if it is very low you will not hear anything and if it is too high you will not hear anything again due to noise. Similarly in the portable fish finders you need to set the sensitivity very carefully. If it is set to very low, you won’t see the fish and if it is very high, you will see the distraction but not a fish.

So, set the sensitivity as high as you can get it without saturating the whole screen with noise. The best way to set the sensitivity is to move your boat and try to see the screen while increasing or decreasing the sensitivity and set where you think the view is good enough.

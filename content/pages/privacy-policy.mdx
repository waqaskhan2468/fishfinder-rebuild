This Privacy Policy explains what information Best Fish Finders Today ("we", "us", "this site") collects when you visit bestfishfinderstoday.com, and how it's used.

## Information We Collect

**Information you provide directly.** If you email us or use a contact form on the site, we collect whatever you send us — typically your name, email address, and message content — solely to respond to your inquiry.

**Automatically collected information.** Like most websites, our hosting and analytics tools automatically log standard technical data when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed, and referring URL. This is used in aggregate to understand site traffic and isn't used to personally identify you.

**Cookies.** This site may use cookies from:

- **Amazon Associates** — to track purchases made through our affiliate links, so we can earn a commission on qualifying purchases (see our [Disclaimer](/desclaimer/)).
- **Analytics providers** (e.g., Google Analytics or similar) — to understand how visitors use the site, in aggregate and anonymized form where possible.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Doing so may affect how affiliate links function but won't prevent you from reading site content.

## How We Use Information

We use the information above to:

- Operate and improve the site and its content
- Respond to questions and messages sent through the Contact page
- Understand aggregate traffic patterns (which pages are useful, how visitors find us)
- Track affiliate referrals to Amazon for commission purposes

## What We Don't Do

We do not sell your personal information. We don't require you to create an account or log in to read this site, and we don't knowingly collect more information than described above.

## Third-Party Services

This site is hosted on Vercel and links out to Amazon.com for product purchases. Those services have their own privacy policies governing data they collect when you interact with them directly — we encourage you to review [Amazon's Privacy Notice](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=468496) if you have questions about purchases made through our links.

## Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your Choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings, and you're always welcome to [contact us](/contact/) if you have questions about data we may hold related to a message you've sent us, or would like it removed.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
